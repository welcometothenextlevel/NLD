The question everyone asks

"How will people find me on Google?"

It's the first thing everyone asks. Here's the honest answer.

Google is how people find you when they're already looking for you. That's your Google Business Profile and your map listing, and we set that up properly as part of your website. It works, but it builds slowly, and it only reaches people who are already searching.

Facebook and Instagram are how people find you when they weren't looking yet. That's where new customers come from in your first month, not your first six months. Your website is where they land. The advertising is what sends them there.

A website without visitors is a business card. The advertising is what brings people to the door.
