What we actually do.

We put a short video or a photo of your work in front of people nearby who are the kind of customer you want. If they're interested, they message you or fill in a quick form — and that lands straight in your phone as a notification.

You don't touch a computer, you don't learn any software, and you don't write the ads. We write them, design them, and watch how they're doing every week. You just answer the phone when it rings.

Honestly

What a realistic first month looks like.

Week one, we set everything up properly — your website connected, tracking in place, and the first ads written and live. Week two and three is where we learn what works: which photo, which offer, which words make people message you. Some ads will do better than others. That's normal, and it's why we watch them closely instead of setting and forgetting.

By the end of the month, most clients have a steady trickle of real enquiries and a clear idea of what each one is costing them. It's rarely instant, and we won't tell you it is — but it moves fast compared to waiting for Google to notice you.

The question everyone asks

"How will people find me on Google?"

It's the first thing everyone asks. Here's the honest answer.

Google is how people find you when they're already looking for you. That's your Google Business Profile and your map listing, and we set that up properly as part of your website. It works, but it builds slowly, and it only reaches people who are already searching.

Facebook and Instagram are how people find you when they weren't looking yet. That's where new customers come from in your first month, not your first six months. Your website is where they land. The advertising is what sends them there.

A website without visitors is a business card. The advertising is what brings people to the door.
