/ The process

Four steps, no surprises.

You always know exactly where the project is, what happens next, and what it will cost. Here's the whole thing, start to finish.

1 Discuss your needs

First, we work out what the website is actually for.

We start with a conversation — not a quote form. We want to understand your business properly before anybody designs anything.

What your business does — the services, the area you cover, who your customers actually are. How many pages you need — and, just as importantly, how many you don't. The goal of the site — an online store, a booking system, quote requests, phone calls, or simply looking credible when someone Googles you.

Takes about 15 minutes. Free, and there's nothing to sign.

Discovery call · brief 15 min
What does your business do, and where do your customers come from right now? Mobile mechanic, western suburbs. Mostly word of mouth — I want people to find me online. How many pages? And what should the site get people to do? Four or five. I want them to book a job or call me.
Brief locked 5 pages · bookings + calls
2 Free sample in 24 hours

We build it for free — and you see it the next day.

Within 24 hours you get a link to a real, working website built for your business. Not a template with your logo dropped on it. Not a PDF. The actual thing, live in your browser, on your phone, ready to click through.

This is how you judge us. You see the quality of our work, the writing, the speed, the way it looks on a phone — all before deciding whether to spend anything at all.

3 Agree and refine

You tell us what to change. Then we agree, in writing.

If you like what you see, we go through your edits together — wording, colours, photos, an extra page, a booking form, whatever the sample made you think of.

Only then do we agree on the scope and a fixed price. No hourly billing, no scope creep, no invoice that turns up bigger than the quote. Once we shake on it, we start building the official version with your edits in place.
